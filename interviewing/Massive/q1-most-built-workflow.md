# Massive role page — Q1 (2026-10-20)

> Which kind of workflow have you built the most times, and roughly how many times? List 3 of those builds: what each one automated, the platform or code you used, and what broke or needed special handling.

---

Full outbound: pull leads from several sources, enrich, score against the client's ICP, route each one to a sequence, a rep or a hold, send, classify replies, and sync to the CRM. Dozens of builds since 2023, plus a few hundred campaign-level reconfigurations. Three of them:

1. Wholesale retailer prospecting (2025–2026). Automated sourcing 240,000+ retailers from public data, enriching them, finding emails (72% discovery), and scoring them into send cohorts. Python and SQL on Supabase/Postgres, with Serper and Bright Data for enrichment. What broke: the enrichment step added a record to its "completed" checkpoint even when the API call failed, to avoid retrying. During an SSL outage, 551 brands were marked complete with zero data and would have been skipped on every future run. I split "tried and failed" from "done" and reconciled the checkpoint against the rows that actually landed. Bright Data jobs also needed special handling: they return asynchronously, so polling had to gate on job status and mark 404s instead of treating them as empty results.

2. Smartlead to Close CRM sync (Feb 2026). Automated moving every outbound lead and reply out of a 150+ mailbox Smartlead setup into Close, with each reply category mapped to a CRM lead status. JavaScript and Python against the Smartlead API and the Close REST API, with Supabase as the staging store. What broke was silent: custom fields came back null, field names differed between campaigns, and state codes arrived in mixed formats. Nothing errored; leads landed with blanks. I added field-name fallbacks and normalization, kept contact-level fields off the lead object, excluded subsequence campaigns so leads didn't import twice, and added request timeouts after imports stalled with no error.

3. Residential-solar lead routing (Jul–Aug 2026). Automated finding homeowners likely to buy solar: 148,759 parcels across 12 markets, enriched with roof fit, scored on permit history from 11 city and county permit portals, skip-traced, then sent and tracked. Python on Supabase, with BatchData, the Google Solar API, Smartlead and Twilio. Special handling: every permit portal is different (Socrata, Accela, EnerGov, ArcGIS), and one blocked automated access, so it needed its own transport. Scoring had to rank within each market rather than across all 12, or one dense city swallowed the send list. Before anything went out, every message passed a deterministic banned-claims check (solar marketing has regulated claims), and sending had a test-send path and a kill switch. Calls came in on tracked numbers so replies could be attributed back to the campaign. 672 tests.
