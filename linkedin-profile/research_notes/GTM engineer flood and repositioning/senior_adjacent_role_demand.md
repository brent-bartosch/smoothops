# Senior role categories adjacent to "GTM Engineer": demand, comp, and title strings (US, Sept–Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-02. All counts below are vendor-reported unless marked "measured". I did not run my own LinkedIn Jobs searches (no authenticated access from this session); that is a gap flagged in each section. Anything older than mid-2025 is marked "(older)".

## Key question 1: Posting volume, exact title strings, seniority, comp bands, and company types per category

### Takeaway
The senior GTM-engineering market above the IC pool is real but thin: trackers put head/director-titled GTM Engineering roles at roughly 2% of ~250 GTM-engineer postings per 30 days (about 6 roles in the last 30 days, ~3 a month earlier), while the adjacent "GTM Systems" director/senior-director track (reporting to VP RevOps) is where the larger, better-paid seats ($200K–$330K base) actually sit. FDE is an order of magnitude larger (~1,200–1,300 live postings) and pays more ($203K median posted base; $243K at frontier labs), but the "FDE for GTM" sub-variant is a handful of postings at seed/Series A companies at $125K–$200K OTE.

### Inferences
- The Head/Director "GTM Engineering" seat exists but is rare: ~3–6 titled postings per 30 days across the whole tracked market, concentrated at AI-infra and dev-tool companies (Deepgram, Fireworks, 6sense, Vercel, Samsara). It is not yet a standard org seat; most companies post exactly one IC GTM Engineer and nothing above it.
- The volume and money at senior level sit under the "GTM Systems" string (Director/Sr Director/Head of GTM Systems), which is the RevOps/business-systems lineage renamed, reports to VP RevOps, and pays $200K–$330K base at Series C+/public companies. Anthropic, LaunchDarkly, Checkr, ServiceTitan, 360Learning all used it in 2026.
- A third cluster is forming around "Revenue Engineering" / "Head of GTM AI and Engineering" (Nooks, Samsara) that pays highest ($260K–$350K base) but is explicitly an engineering-management seat.
- FDE comp dominance comes from frontier labs (~10% of FDE postings); FDE-GTM at startups pays like a senior SE/CSM ($125K–$200K OTE), not like a lab FDE.

## Key question 2: Growing vs shrinking into Q4 2026; is FDE still expanding; is "Head of GTM Engineering" a real seat?

### Takeaway
FDE is still expanding in absolute terms (1,276 live postings at end-Oct, up from ~1,262 on Oct 18 and 406–496 on narrower trackers in Sept), and OpenAI/Anthropic keep adding FDE and Applied AI postings across geographies; GTM Engineer volume is flat-to-slightly-up (~+4% on overlapping 30-day windows) while sales/RevOps leadership postings contracted sharply in Aug→Sep 2026 (head-of-function −27%, director −15%). ## Key question 3: What senior postings screen for; which are credibly claimable by a non-SWE-by-title operator; which require CS/coding interviews

### Takeaway
There are two distinct gates. "GTM Systems" and "AI enablement/RevOps" leadership seats screen on years of RevOps/Sales Ops leadership, Salesforce/CPQ/quote-to-cash depth, people management, and (increasingly) hands-on AI automation; none of the verified JDs require a CS degree or coding interview. "Director GTM Engineering", "Director Revenue Engineering", "Head of GTM AI and Engineering" and all FDE seats require professional software engineering (production code, Python/LLM stack) and, at lab level, are effectively SWE hiring loops.

### Cited Findings
